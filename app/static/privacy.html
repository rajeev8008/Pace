Pace privacy policy

Pace uses Google or GitHub sign-in only to identify you and create your account. It stores the profile details returned by your chosen provider together with the tasks, goals, preferences, and email schedule you add.

Your information is used only to provide Pace features. Pace does not sell your data. Email notifications are sent through Resend when you enable them.

You can stop using Pace at any time and revoke its access from your Google or GitHub account settings. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact k.rajeev8008@gmail.com.
